Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Recall Radar ("the app," "we") helps you check whether things you own have been recalled. We built it to need as little of your information as possible.

What we don't collect

You don't need an account. We don't ask for your name, email, phone number, or location. We don't sell or share personal information, and we don't use advertising trackers.

Your list stays on your device

The items you add (for example a car model, a medicine name, or a product type) are saved only in your browser's storage on your device. We can't see your list. If you clear your browser data or uninstall the app, your list is deleted.

How recall checks work

To check for recalls, the app sends the item you entered (such as "2019 Honda Accord" or "crib") to public U.S. government databases: the National Highway Traffic Safety Administration (NHTSA), the U.S. Food and Drug Administration (openFDA), and the U.S. Consumer Product Safety Commission (CPSC). If you enter a VIN, it is sent to NHTSA's public VIN decoder only to look up your vehicle's year, make, and model. These agencies receive standard web request information, such as your IP address, under their own privacy policies. In some cases the request passes through our hosting provider's server first, which does not store it.

Other services

The app loads fonts from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address. Our website host keeps standard server logs (such as IP address and pages visited) for security and reliability. If you buy Pro, payment is handled by our payment provider (for example Stripe), which collects the information needed to process your payment under its own privacy policy. We receive only what's needed to confirm your purchase.

Children

The app is meant for adults. It does not knowingly collect information from children.

If we change this policy, we'll update the date above.
