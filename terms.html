What Recall Radar is

Recall Radar is an independent tool that searches public recall records published by U.S. government agencies (NHTSA, FDA, and CPSC). It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by any of these agencies or by any manufacturer.

Important limits

  • Recall information can be delayed, incomplete, or worded differently than what you search for. A result of "All clear" does not guarantee that an item has no recalls.
  • A match by name does not mean your specific item is affected. Always compare model numbers, lot numbers, sizes, and dates with the official notice.
  • For vehicles, results cover the model, not your exact car. Use your VIN on the official NHTSA website or contact a dealer to confirm open recalls for your vehicle.
  • The app does not give medical, legal, or mechanical advice. Do not stop taking a prescribed medicine without talking to your doctor or pharmacist.
  • Always follow the instructions in the official recall notice and from the manufacturer.
